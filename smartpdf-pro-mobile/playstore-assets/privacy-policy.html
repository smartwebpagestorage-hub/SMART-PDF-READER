Privacy Policy for Smart PDF Pro

Last updated: November 2026

Summary: Smart PDF Pro does NOT collect, transmit, store, or sell any personal data, documents, or analytics. Everything runs 100% locally on your device.

1. Information We Do NOT Collect

Smart PDF Pro is designed with complete privacy and zero data collection as its core foundation:

  • No Personal Information: We do not ask for or collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or accounts.
  • No Document Data: Any PDF document you open, read, edit, annotate, split, or merge is processed entirely within your device's memory. No document is ever uploaded to any server or cloud service.
  • No Analytics or Tracking: The app does not include tracking SDKs, telemetry, crash-reporting analytics, or behavioral trackers.
  • No Advertising: The app is 100% ad-free. No ad networks or identifiers are used.

2. Device Permissions Usage

Smart PDF Pro requests only the minimal permissions required to provide PDF viewing and editing functionality:

  • File Access / Storage: Required solely to open PDFs selected by the user and save edited or merged PDF documents directly to the device's storage. All file operations are user-initiated via standard Android system document pickers.
  • Internet Permission: Used only for the local in-app WebView sandbox environment to render HTML5 viewer components. No outbound communication or telemetry is performed.

3. Third-Party Services

Smart PDF Pro does not integrate third-party ad networks, tracking platforms, or external data brokers.

4. Children's Privacy

Because Smart PDF Pro collects zero personal information, it complies fully with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play's Families Policy.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

If we update this Privacy Policy in future versions, updates will be reflected directly on this page with an updated revision date.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer via the official support channel or GitHub repository.
